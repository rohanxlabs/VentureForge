# Evaluation prompts

Use the same prompts after each milestone.

1. Climate/public safety: Research the operational challenges of landslide early-warning systems in Northeast India.
2. Healthcare: Research workflow challenges rural clinics face coordinating follow-up care for patients with chronic conditions.
3. Developer tools: Research operational challenges small engineering teams face diagnosing production incidents across distributed services.

These are evaluation fixtures, not validated findings.

## Shared checklist
- Source text and metadata are saved for cited sources.
- Every verified quote exists exactly in captured text.
- Citations outside the current run fail validation.
- Facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unknowns are distinguishable.
- Unsupported claims are not presented as facts.
- Findings trace to question, passage, and source.
